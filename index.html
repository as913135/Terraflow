Know what's under the road before the JCB arrives.

TerraFlow maps Manali's buried water, sewer and fiber lines in 2D and 3D, scores every segment for failure risk from records, rainfall, slope and citizen reports — so crews inspect the right pipe first. No sensors, no new hardware.

Operational advisory — TerraFlow calculates failure vulnerability & inspection priority from historical records, rainfall saturation, terrain slope and community reports. It does not confirm active leaks or certify site safety. High-risk segments require physical verification with Jal Shakti Vibhag (JSV) / MC Manali / HPSEBL and a municipal excavation permit before mechanical digging.
